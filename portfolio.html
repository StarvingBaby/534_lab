A CLASSROOM THOUGHT EXPERIMENT

Does less risk mean less return?

Two assets can rise independently. Combining them sometimes lowers volatility without proportionally reducing expected returns. But the precise tradeoff depends on historical correlation.

Correlation matters: Portfolio variance includes both individual variances and their covariance. Changing weight changes portfolio volatility in a non-linear way.

Sharpe matters: We compare excess arithmetic returns per unit of volatility. Choose a time horizon and see if the historical best mix stays the same.

Watch the assumptions: Returns and volatility are computed from monthly historical data. They are not known future expectations.

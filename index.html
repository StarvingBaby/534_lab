How this works
01 / Historical prices

SPY and GLD adjusted ETF closing prices, sourced from Yahoo Finance using an automated refresh.

02 / Monthly returns

Each point is the percentage change between successive month-end adjusted prices. Months are aligned by shared trading dates.

03 / OLS regression

We fit GLD return = α + β × SPY return. Correlation does not imply causation or guarantee downside protection.

04 / Portfolio concepts

Volatility is the standard deviation of monthly returns × √12. Sharpe uses an editable annual risk-free assumption. Drawdown is measured from monthly observations.

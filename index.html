03 / SHOW YOUR WORK

Sources & methodology.

Yield-source fallback: if FRED is unavailable after retries, the refresh uses the U.S. Treasury’s official daily par yield curve, field BC_10YEAR, for the entire history. It is an explicitly documented alternative to DGS10. The actual provider is shown in the month readout and retrieval details; providers are never mixed within a refresh. The duration calculation uses the selected source’s 10-year yield in percent.

Three series, three distinct definitions

New York Fed ACM Treasury Term Premia: official workbook, sheet ACM Monthly, columns DATE and ACMTP10. End-of-month model estimates in percent annualized; estimates can be revised and are not a traded price.

FRED DGS10: daily 10-year constant-maturity Treasury yield, percent. We use the last available yield in each completed month, retaining its actual date.

GLD via Yahoo Finance / yfinance: existing adjusted closing prices (auto_adjust=True), last common SPY/GLD trading date per month. Adjustments reflect splits/distributions as supplied by the vendor. GLD is an ETF proxy for gold, not spot gold; returns exclude personal taxes and trading costs.

Duration calculation & rate-shock units

Face value 100; maturity 10 years; 20 semiannual cash flows. Set annual coupon rate equal to the selected 10-year yield / 100. Discount each coupon and final principal at (1 + y/2)t. Price is the sum of present values. Macaulay duration is their time-weighted mean in years; modified duration divides it by (1 + y/2). This is a hypothetical model proxy, not a measured CUSIP duration.

For a +100 bps shock, Δy = +0.01 and ΔP/P ≈ −Dmod × 0.01. This ignores convexity, coupon reinvestment, roll-down, and nonparallel yield-curve changes.

Alignment, window selection & reproducibility

Only completed months present in all three sources are used; missing months are never filled. ACM, yield, and GLD may have different observation dates within the same calendar month. The finding explicitly reports those endpoint dates. GLD return is (end adjusted close / start adjusted close − 1) × 100. Term-premium change is (end % − start %) × 100 basis points.

The initial finding scans all consecutive six-calendar-month windows and chooses the largest absolute ACM change, breaking ties by earliest start. GLD does not influence selection. This retrospective extreme is deliberately illustrative and can exaggerate typical movements; it cannot establish causality, a dependable hedge, or future performance. User-selected windows recompute the same formulas.
